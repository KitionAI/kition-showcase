How do I borrow an e-book? −

Open the title, choose Borrow and it appears on your shelf straight away. Loans last 21 days.

Can I read offline? +

Yes. Download a book while connected and it stays readable until the loan ends.

Is membership free? +

Yes, for anyone who lives, works or studies in the county.

Why was I charged a late fee? +

Physical items returned after their due date cost 20p a day, up to £3 per item. E-books return themselves, so they never cost anything.

How do I pay a fee? +

At any branch desk, or online under Your account.

I forgot my PIN +

Use Reset PIN on the sign-in page; a new one goes to your email.
