# Lakeside Camping Checklist

Three nights at Loch Arrin by canoe, 14 to 17 May. Everything must fit in two dry bags and the barrel.

## Day before

- [x] Check the forecast and the wind on the loch
- [x] Leave the route with Morag
- [ ] Charge the phone and the power bank
- [x] Seal the map in its case

## Dry bag 1: shelter

- [x] Tent, poles and pegs
    - [x] Spare pegs for the sandy pitch
    - [ ] Repair sleeve
- [x] Two sleeping bags (rated to 2 °C)
- [x] Sleeping mats and pump sack
- [ ] Tarp and 20 m of cord

## Dry bag 2: clothes

- [x] Waterproof jackets and trousers
- [x] Fleece and a warm hat each
- [x] Spare socks in their own bag
- [ ] Sandals for the shore

## Barrel: kitchen

- [x] Stove, two gas canisters and a lighter
- [x] Pan set, mugs and sporks
- [ ] Water filter
    - [ ] Spare cartridge
- [ ] Food for four days, breakfasts bagged by day
- [ ] Rubbish bags: pack it all out
